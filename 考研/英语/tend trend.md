你单独发 **tendency**，我接着上面补充。它其实是 **tend 的名词形式**，考研英一里常考“倾向、趋势”。

### 一、tendency 核心含义
**tendency** /ˈtendənsi/  
**n. [C] 倾向；趋势；癖性；习惯性倾向**

它是**可数名词**，常用 **a/the tendency**，复数 **tendencies**。

### 二、常用搭配
1. **a tendency to do sth**
   有做某事的倾向；往往做某事  
   - People have a **tendency to** believe what they want to believe.  
     人们往往相信他们愿意相信的东西。  
   - She has a **tendency to** exaggerate.  
     她有夸大其词的倾向。

2. **have a tendency to do sth**
   = tend to do sth  
   - He **has a tendency to** arrive late.  
     = He **tends to** arrive late.  
     他往往迟到。

3. **a tendency towards / to sth**
   朝向……的倾向/趋势  
   - a **tendency towards** inflation  
     通货膨胀趋势  
   - a **tendency towards** conservatism  
     保守主义倾向

4. **There is a tendency for sb to do sth**
   有……的倾向  
   - **There is a growing tendency for** students to study abroad.  
     学生出国留学的趋势日益增长。

5. **a growing / increasing / general tendency**
   日益增长/普遍的趋势

6. **reverse / curb / check a tendency**
   扭转/抑制某种趋势

### 三、tendency 与 tend、trend 的关系
- **tend** 是动词：  
  - tend to do sth = 往往做某事  
  - tend towards sth = 倾向于……
- **tendency** 是名词：  
  - have a tendency to do sth = tend to do sth
- **trend** 是名词/动词：  
  - 趋势、潮流、动向  
  - a trend towards sth；an upward/downward trend

### 四、tendency 和 trend 的区别
| 对比 | tendency | trend |
|---|---|---|
| 词性 | 名词 | 名词/动词 |
| 含义 | 倾向；趋势；癖性 | 趋势；潮流；总体动向 |
| 搭配 | tendency to do；tendency towards sth | trend towards/in；upward trend |
| 侧重点 | 内在倾向、习惯性倾向 | 外部宏观发展趋势 |
| 可数 | 可数 | 可数 |
| 图表作文 | 可用，但不如 trend 常用 | 更常用：show an upward trend |

例句对比：
- He has a **tendency** to be late.  
  他有迟到的倾向。  
- There is a growing **trend** towards online learning.  
  在线学习呈增长趋势。

### 五、考研英一易错点
- ❌ a tendency doing sth  
  ✅ a tendency to do sth

- ❌ There is a tend that...  
  ✅ There is a tendency for sb to do sth  
  ✅ There is a tendency to do sth

- ❌ People trend to do...  
  ✅ People tend to do...  
  ✅ People have a tendency to do...

- **tendency** 可数，不要裸用：  
  ❌ show tendency  
  ✅ show a tendency

### 六、阅读同义替换
**tend to do** 常被替换为：
- have a tendency to do
- be likely to do
- be inclined to do
- generally / usually

**tendency** 常被替换为：
- inclination
- propensity
- trend
- drift

### 一句话总结
**tend 是动词“往往、倾向于”；tendency 是名词“倾向、趋势”；trend 是名词“趋势、潮流”。**  
考研写作记住：**have a tendency to do / there is a growing tendency for sb to do**。
